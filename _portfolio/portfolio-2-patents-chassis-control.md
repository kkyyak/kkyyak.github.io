A portfolio of verified intellectual property assigned to Astemo, Ltd. (formerly Hitachi Astemo, Ltd. / Hitachi Automotive Systems, Ltd.), where Jian Wang is an official inventor:

* **US Patent 12,691,853 B2** (*Braking Support Control Device*; Pub. US 2025/0187569 A1; Family: PCT/JP2022/031918 / DE112022006401.3T): Adaptive AEB threshold modulation based on real-time future prediction accuracy.
* **US Patent 12,466,366 B2** (*Automatic Emergency Braking Device*): AEB activation timing optimization during oncoming and opposite-lane entry scenarios.
* **JP Patent 7833556 B2 / US Pub. US 2026/0028021 A1** (*Vehicle Control Device*; Family: WO 2024/069728 A1 / EP 4597470 A4): Vehicle position estimation and curved/turning-trajectory control.
* **US Patent 12,179,835 B2** (*Vehicle Coupling Assistance Device*): Multi-wheel steering mode control for trailer hitching and coupling assistance.
