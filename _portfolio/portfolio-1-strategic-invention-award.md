Awarded the prestigious **Strategic Invention Award (Bronze Prize)** by Hitachi Astemo, Ltd. on November 16, 2023, for breakthrough algorithmic contributions to millimeter-wave radar stationary obstacle detection (PCT/JP2023/007257 / WO 2023/162234). 

The algorithm effectively suppresses road-structure false alarms while reliably triggering emergency braking assistance for genuine hazards.
