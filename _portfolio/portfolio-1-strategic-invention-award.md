Awarded the prestigious **Strategic Invention Award (Bronze Prize)** by Hitachi Astemo, Ltd. on November 16, 2023, for breakthrough algorithmic contributions to millimeter-wave radar stationary obstacle detection (**PCT/JP2023/007257 / WO 2023/162234**) and its associated multi-sensor external recognition architecture (**US Patent 12,694,689 B2**).

The invention pair effectively resolves heterogeneous sensor latency synchronization and suppresses road-structure false alarms while reliably triggering emergency braking assistance for genuine hazards.
