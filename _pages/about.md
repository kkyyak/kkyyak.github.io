I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Informatics at **The Graduate University for Advanced Studies (SOKENDAI)** and **National Institute of Informatics (NII)**, Tokyo, Japan, working in the [Echizen Laboratory](https://research.nii.ac.jp/~iechizen/official/) under the supervision of **Prof. Isao Echizen**. Concurrently, I am an R&D Engineer at **Astemo, Ltd.** (formerly Hitachi Astemo / Hitachi Automotive Systems).

Research Interests
======
My doctoral research lies at the intersection of **Computer Vision, Cyber-Physical Security, and Autonomous Driving (AD)**. With the rapid evolution of autonomous systems, the vulnerability of camera sensors to physical and optical adversarial attacks poses an existential safety threat to automated vehicles. 

Specifically, my research focuses on:
* **Sensor Attack Surface Analysis**: Investigating vulnerabilities of vehicular camera sensors against physical adversarial patches, optical projections, and dynamic illumination interference.
* **Spatio-Temporal Consistency Modeling**: Formulating physics-constrained and time-series consistency defense mechanisms that leverage ego-motion dynamics and temporal scene constraints to reject malicious perturbations.
* **Robust Autonomous Perception**: Designing real-time, low-latency defense frameworks that seamlessly integrate into automotive-grade perception architectures.

Industry Background & Achievements
======
Prior to and alongside my doctoral studies, I have dedicated over a decade to core automotive chassis control and active safety systems at Hitachi / Astemo. My industrial track record includes:
* **Hitachi Astemo Strategic Invention Award (Bronze Prize, 2023)** for algorithmic and R&D contributions to millimeter-wave radar stationary obstacle detection and false-alarm suppression.
* **4 Granted and Published Patent Families (JP/US/PCT)** covering adaptive emergency braking modulated by prediction accuracy, oncoming-lane AEB timing optimization, curved/turning-trajectory vehicle control, and multi-wheel steering trailer coupling assistance.
* **Automotive Engineering Publications** in the *Transactions of the Society of Automotive Engineers of Japan (JSAE)* and SICE.
