### Authors
**Jian Wang**, Taku Takahama (Hitachi Automotive Systems, Ltd.)

### Abstract
Accurate real-time knowledge of the relative yaw angle (articulation angle) between the tow vehicle and the trailer is paramount for autonomous and assisted trailer hitching. This work formulates an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) observer based on non-linear kinematic vehicle-trailer models to filter out measurement noises and dynamically track the relative hitch angle during maneuvering.
