**Jian Wang**, Taku Takahama (Hitachi Automotive Systems, Ltd.)

### Abstract
Coupling a passenger vehicle with a trailer (hitching) is a challenging task with extensive blind spots that traditionally requires significant driving skill or an external ground guide. This paper presents an automatic hitching assist steering control system adopting four-wheel-steering (4WS). By adaptively coordinating in-phase and anti-phase steering angles of the front and rear wheels according to the relative displacement and angle to the trailer hitch, the system achieves smooth and intuitive target alignment without driver maneuvering burden.
